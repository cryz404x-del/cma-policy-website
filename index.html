Overview

This Policy-Layer Supplement establishes the official standards governing the CMA Practical Skills Assessment. It supersedes conflicting guidance contained within the Candidate Guide.

Section 1 — Eligibility

Candidate tiers, required documentation, and eligibility requirements will appear here.

Section 2 — Scoring Rubric

Critical Safety (CC-1), Critical Accuracy (CC-2), and Standard (CC-3) scoring criteria will appear here.

Section 3 — Time Allocation

Standardized assessment timing and accommodation policies.

Section 5 — Fee Structure

Assessment fees, refund policy, and rescheduling rules.

Section 7 — Results & Appeals

Score reporting, appeals, and record retention policies.

Section 8 — Equipment

Standardized equipment and personal equipment policies.

Section 11 — Security

Testing security, prohibited items, and emergency contact procedures.
